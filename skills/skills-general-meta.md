# 1. General & Meta Skills (GS‑001–GS‑050)

1. **GS‑001 – Structured General Assistant** – Default Claude assistant that responds clearly, step‑by‑step, and asks for clarification when needed.
2. **GS‑002 – Socratic Questioner** – Guides the user by asking probing questions instead of giving direct answers immediately.
3. **GS‑003 – Clarifying Requirements Bot** – Specializes in rewriting vague tasks into precise, testable requirements.
4. **GS‑004 – Task Decomposer** – Breaks complex goals into ordered subtasks with dependencies and milestones.
5. **GS‑005 – Planning & Execution Orchestrator** – Creates a multi‑phase plan, then executes one phase at a time with checkpoints.
6. **GS‑006 – Constraint Enforcer** – Ensures all outputs obey explicit constraints like word limits, formats, or style rules.
7. **GS‑007 – Safety & Red‑Team Checker** – Reviews ideas and prompts for safety, privacy, and policy concerns before execution.
8. **GS‑008 – Persona Switchboard** – Suggests and manages different personas (teacher, critic, coach) for a given task.
9. **GS‑009 – Prompt Optimizer** – Takes a rough prompt and rewrites it into a clearer, more structured version.
10. **GS‑010 – Output Evaluator** – Scores and critiques Claude’s own answer quality against explicit criteria.
11. **GS‑011 – XML‑Tag Response Formatter** – Wraps responses in custom XML tags for easy downstream parsing.
12. **GS‑012 – JSON Schema Filler** – Fills a given JSON schema strictly, refusing to add extra fields.
13. **GS‑013 – Markdown Formatter** – Ensures all responses are clean, well‑structured Markdown (headings, lists, tables).
14. **GS‑014 – Role‑Play Controller** – Maintains a role‑play persona while still complying with system constraints.
15. **GS‑015 – Thought‑Trace Summarizer** – Produces a concise summary of internal reasoning without exposing raw chain‑of‑thought.
16. **GS‑016 – “Think Step by Step” Enforcer** – Always structures solutions as numbered reasoning steps.
17. **GS‑017 – Multi‑Option Generator** – Always proposes 3–5 distinct options with pros and cons.
18. **GS‑018 – Decision Helper** – Helps pick between options using explicit decision criteria and scoring.
19. **GS‑019 – Assumption Highlighter** – Flags hidden assumptions in user questions and proposed solutions.
20. **GS‑020 – Risk & Edge‑Case Spotter** – Lists failure modes, edge cases, and ways to mitigate them.
21. **GS‑021 – Meta‑Critic** – Critiques its own previous answer and proposes a better version.
22. **GS‑022 – Explain‑Like‑I’m‑Five Translator** – Turns complex explanations into very simple, intuitive language.
23. **GS‑023 – Jargon Buster** – Rewrites an explanation with minimal jargon, defining all technical terms.
24. **GS‑024 – Style Mimic** – Imitates a given writing or reasoning style from examples provided by the user.
25. **GS‑025 – Cross‑Cultural Sensitivity Checker** – Reviews content for cultural assumptions and potential offense.
26. **GS‑026 – Error Message Interpreter** – Explains confusing AI or system error messages in plain language.
27. **GS‑027 – Session Memory Summarizer** – Summarizes a long conversation into a reusable brief for future sessions.
28. **GS‑028 – Goal Alignment Checker** – Checks if a proposed plan truly targets the stated user goal.
29. **GS‑029 – Time‑Boxed Planner** – Designs a plan that fits within strict time limits (e.g., 30 minutes).
30. **GS‑030 – Resource‑Aware Planner** – Plans tasks given constraints like skills, tools, and budget.
31. **GS‑031 – Cross‑Model Prompt Portability Advisor** – Suggests how to adapt a prompt for different LLM providers.
32. **GS‑032 – Localization Advisor** – Points out what needs changing to adapt content for another locale or language.
33. **GS‑033 – Content Policy Sanity Checker** – Simulates a content‑policy review of a draft.
34. **GS‑034 – Multi‑Persona Debate Moderator** – Orchestrates a debate among multiple named Claude personas.
35. **GS‑035 – Priority Negotiator** – Helps users trade off priorities like cost, speed, and quality.
36. **GS‑036 – Iterative Refinement Partner** – Always proposes an improved next iteration, then asks for feedback.
37. **GS‑037 – “Rubber Duck” Listener** – Lets user talk through a problem and mirrors it back without prematurely solving.
38. **GS‑038 – Constraints‑First Designer** – Starts by listing constraints, then designs within them.
39. **GS‑039 – Analogy Maker** – Explains a concept with multiple analogies tailored to user background.
40. **GS‑040 – Output Length Controller** – Keeps responses within a target token/word range.
41. **GS‑041 – Format‑Aware Router** – Chooses between list, table, narrative, or diagram depending on task.
42. **GS‑042 – Multi‑Language Switcher** – Can switch languages mid‑conversation while preserving technical accuracy.
43. **GS‑043 – Critique‑Then‑Create Pattern** – First critiques existing work, then uses critique to create a better version.
44. **GS‑044 – Traceability Enforcer** – Ensures every recommendation points back to explicit evidence or assumptions.
45. **GS‑045 – Checklist Generator** – Converts complex procedures into actionable checklists.
46. **GS‑046 – API Output Post‑Processor** – Cleans and structures raw tool/API output for human consumption.
47. **GS‑047 – Conversation Boundary Setter** – Clarifies what will and won’t be covered in a session.
48. **GS‑048 – Feedback Summarizer** – Aggregates multiple pieces of feedback into themes and action items.
49. **GS‑049 – Logging & Note‑Taking Assistant** – Turns a live discussion into structured notes or minutes.
50. **GS‑050 – Meta‑Prompt Designer** – Helps design custom prompts and skill definitions for this very skills bank.
